## Source-qualification result and next decision

The bounded review found bulk report-period summaries, but their inspected
headers lack file IDs and amendment chains. Daily original archives exist; they
are not a ready-made processed history source. OpenFEC's processed filing/report
endpoints supply useful metadata but disagree on status assertions and return
some values in types that differ from the pinned schema. No inspected source
alone proves that an attachment is a complete financial replacement.

The accepted additional source is a **separate report-metadata API capture**, not a
transaction fallback. The local Go reader and bounded fetcher implement the
descriptor and assertion-preservation boundary. Do not substitute
date/amount joins or download all originals to fill unresolved history.
The [source catalog](./source-catalog.md) remains authoritative for recurring
source selection; processed A/B/E remain the detailed ledgers.

A candidate metadata source must preserve:

- Exact publisher file/report ID, committee ID, physical source identity, and
  capture revision; electronic versus paper origin and declared format.
- Declared form and report kind, report year, coverage start/end, and whether
  a financial cover is actually present. Missing fields stay missing.
- Publisher amendment/original-file links and latest/amended assertions without
  turning those assertions into our selection policy.
- The applicable form/account scope for the field under comparison, or an
  explicit unknown state. A committee ID alone is not account coverage.
- Reports without Schedule A rows, corrections received after the cycle, and
  enough history to expose missing predecessors, conflicts, and attachments.

Here, account scope means the reporting/form/financial-field population, not a
private bank-account number. Preserve federal/nonfederal or other disclosed
reporting distinctions where relevant. A committee ID or a zero amount does not
prove coverage, and unavailable private identifiers must not become an invented
prerequisite for comparing otherwise compatible reported subtotals.

The electronic, paper, zero-Schedule-A, attachment-only, and conflicting-cover
witnesses now pass bounded source checks. The Go capture/assertion contract now:

- Pins each endpoint response and reviewed schema separately, retaining raw
  value types, nulls, negative references, conflicts, and observation times.
- Separates reported predecessor/status assertions from accepted report-family
  and financial-selection decisions; neither endpoint has automatic precedence.
- Reports bounded pagination evidence without claiming refreshed old assertions.
  Receipt-date-only polling cannot detect all changes to older report status.
  Cycle filters do not bound when amendments may arrive.
- Keeps metadata snapshots separate from bulk release ancestry. A shared Monday
  run is not proof of a common publisher snapshot.

The pinned fixtures and retained full endpoint pages pass the local Go gate.
Full history cost, pagination consistency, and refresh reconciliation remain
acceptance gates before large or recurring runs.

## Selection and comparison invariants

1. Bind each future comparison to one exact summary assertion and explicit
   detailed form/line population. An asserted period subtotal and an accepted
   cash measure are different outputs.
2. Preserve physical occurrences. Detect repeated publisher keys and ambiguous
   report-local identities before counting; do not deduplicate paper rows by
   name/date/amount or assume original transaction IDs exist for paper.
3. Preserve amendment relationships and conflicting versions. Selection must
   distinguish a complete financial replacement from an attachment. Neither
   largest file number, processing action code, receipt date, nor a latest flag
   is sufficient on its own.
4. Retain declared reporting intervals independently of receipt dates. Identify
   gaps, overlaps, missing reports, and source-cutoff differences against an
   explicit account/period scope. Do not force every committee to have activity
   for the full cycle or infer a zero report from absence of receipts.
5. Preserve source disagreements. Memo-box evidence, raw memo code, and reported
   cover arithmetic can disagree. Any accepted interpretation or correction is
   additive, field-scoped, source-located, and versioned; no named runtime repairs.
6. Once population compatibility is established, permit an exact **reported
   subtotal difference**, including a nonzero result. Do not require invented
   repairs to make it zero or label a difference as unitemized receipts.
7. Apply unresolved scope locally: a conflicting report blocks its affected
   comparison, not unrelated evidence publication. Whole-cycle completeness
   still requires every required constituent to have an accepted disposition.

No accepted cycle-summary difference follows from the current eleven-report
sample. All eleven audited covers are marked new, but their effective status
and summary membership remain unverified. Earlier zero-row and attachment-only
witnesses prevent treating the processed Schedule A reference list as complete
report history. Cash timing, in-kind valuation, and terminal allocation remain
separate requirements after reported-subtotal comparison.
