# Go rewrite inventory — 2026-09-20

Status: point-in-time high-level audit of source, retained data, development
services and documentation. This document distinguishes verified implementation
from experiments and intended design. It is not a production-readiness claim.

## Executive result

Legal Tender now has a strong Go FEC evidence and calculation foundation. It
preserves detailed source grain, exact money, source lineage, unresolved states
and calculation conservation more rigorously than the legacy Python exploration.
It also has bounded ArangoDB projections and read-only candidate evidence views.

It is not yet the intended investigative product. The current system lacks an
accepted production identity layer, committee-chain dollar attribution, one
unified product graph, a deployed query/API surface and a user interface. Schedule
A and B detailed processing is complete only for 2024; Schedule E is complete for
all four target cycles. Lobbying and legislative-action evidence are later domains,
not part of the implemented FEC slice.

The next step is not automatically another immutable publisher. The accepted
direct source-appearance calculation is already deterministic and exactly
replayable. Durable publication and Dagster wiring should wait for a concrete
downstream graph, API or scheduled-calculation consumer.

## Durable core

The following behavior is justified independently of any presentation or terminal
policy and should remain:

- immutable acquired source identity and coordinated release identity;
- lossless source occurrences and normalized facts as separate layers;
- integer minor-unit money and explicit unknown values;
- row-level lineage back to publisher evidence;
- conservation gates, exact readback and explicit unresolved populations;
- Go ownership of source, domain, calculation, graph and serving behavior; and
- Python limited to Dagster's control plane.

This is the real rebuild foundation. The earlier recovery and exact-build detour
does not invalidate these evidence contracts.

## Verified work in progress

The worktree is substantially ahead of the last pushed commit. At audit completion
it contains 14 modified tracked files and 47 untracked files. The new layer
includes the accepted pre-attribution interpretation publications, candidate
dossier, terminal-policy comparison and complete direct source-appearance
calculation. The full Go gate passes over this work.

The interpretation publications are retained under project storage. The direct
source-appearance result is still a disposable `/tmp` artifact even though its
eight-worker and four-worker results are byte-identical. That difference is
operational, not a correctness gap in the calculation.

This work should be reviewed and landed as one coherent checkpoint before another
large feature branch is added. Landing it does not require inventing a new
publication or Dagster asset.

## Parked exploration

The relationship, prose extraction and local-model trials remain useful evidence
about what does not yet meet the identity bar. They do not form an accepted runtime
identity pipeline. Additional model trials and autonomous page discovery remain
paused until a specific identity case requires them.

Read-only recovery tools, dependency locks and existing publication-integrity
checks remain available. New archive systems, exact-build campaigns and recovery
expansion are not current product work.

The terminal-policy comparison and two-candidate dossier are review instruments.
They made the missing semantic boundaries visible; they are not themselves the
general product architecture.

## Live development state

The legacy development Compose stack is running: Dagster development services,
ArangoDB 3.11 and PostgreSQL 15. No production cutover has occurred. PostgreSQL is
part of the legacy/development topology; ArangoDB remains the target investigative
domain database.

Rounded `du` measurements show about 584 GiB of Legal Tender data: 497 GiB raw,
55 GiB facts, 32 GiB dumps, 142 MiB calculations, 12 MiB cache and a small bundle
tree. The filesystem has about 1.2 TiB free. Storage pressure does not require a
cleanup or architecture detour now.

## Actual open boundaries

1. **Entity identity and affiliation.** Direct and earmarked appearances still
   name reported source occurrences, not deduplicated people, employers or
   corporate families.
2. **Committee-chain meaning.** Paths and cycles exist, but there is no accepted
   allocation from pooled committee money to upstream economic sources.
3. **Unified product projection.** Existing graph generations prove individual
   models. They do not yet provide one candidate-centered, evidence-backed graph
   and query contract.
4. **Coverage and operation.** Remaining Schedule A/B cycles and a real unattended
   weekly refresh must follow the accepted single-cycle contracts.
5. **Serving and investigation.** The general API and investigative UI do not
   exist yet.
6. **Influence domains.** Lobbying, legislation, votes and other government-action
   evidence remain outside the implemented FEC funding system.

## Corrected sequence

1. Review and land or deliberately remove the September 20 worktree as one
   checkpoint. Do not expand it merely to make every derived result durable.
2. Use the candidate-shaped view to select and measure the next domain boundary.
   The strongest next candidate is deterministic identity resolution for direct
   and earmarked appearances, with unresolved cases preserved.
3. Investigate committee-chain allocation separately. Do not let graph reachability
   imply economic ownership.
4. Add a content-addressed publisher, Dagster asset or ArangoDB projection only
   when an accepted downstream consumer needs that result.
5. After the domain path works end to end, expand Schedule A/B across the target
   cycles, prove unattended weekly refresh and build the general API/UI.

This sequence preserves reproducibility without allowing publication machinery to
substitute for unresolved product semantics.
