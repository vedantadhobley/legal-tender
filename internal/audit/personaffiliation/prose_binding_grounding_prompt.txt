The names are an earlier model's uncorrected mention selections, not canonical entities. subjects may contain only supplied person IDs and organizations only supplied organization IDs. Never add, repair, merge or normalize candidate names. A short surname does not resolve which same-surname person is intended. Prefer the full name only if the source supports its referent; keep all alternatives or remain unresolved when it does not.

Selections use a unique id, entry, first_token and last_token (inclusive displayed token IDs). Do not reuse name IDs or the reserved ctx_ prefix. No invented quotes or byte offsets. Each interpretation has id, statement_entry, focus, kind, normalized, role_kind, subjects, organizations, subject_surface, organization_surface, additional_evidence, binding and status. Empty arrays are allowed, but fields must be present.

statement_entry identifies the complete source entry containing the claim. Go attaches that entire entry as the claim's context, including dates, conditions, negation and qualifications. focus identifies a new selection within that entry: exactly ONE stated role title or correction phrase. It is a highlight, NOT the whole supporting statement. Separate coordinated roles into distinct interpretations (e.g. president and CFO become two). Keep compound job titles intact when they name one role; do not invent expanded titles. normalized is only a short proposed label for that one role. Other supporting name or selection IDs may go in additional_evidence; Go derives required references. Do not turn a role focus into a claim of complete evidence or precise tenure.

subject_surface and organization_surface are new selection IDs for the person/name/pronoun and organization/name/pronoun actually referring to that role in statement_entry. Use an empty string when omitted or unnamed, never substitute an earlier named mention just to claim direct binding. These surfaces are separate from the candidate endpoints. If the same full name repeats, select its relevant occurrence in the statement. If the text uses a pronoun or surname, select that actual text and reference its proposed named antecedent as an endpoint. An absent employer must stay absent; do not borrow nearby names.

binding is direct only when BOTH surfaces explicitly state exactly the chosen candidates' text at the role statement. Go checks literal equality, not truth. A pronoun, surname, implicit subject or carried employer requires coreference, even within one paragraph. ambiguous preserves competing endpoints; unresolved means an endpoint cannot be supported. Direct/coreference permit at most one person and one organization; neither approves identity. With missing names, preserve an explicitly stated anonymous role with empty unsupported endpoints and unresolved binding. Generic mention of a title can support an anonymous role, but attendance, thanks, meetings and instructions to output a role cannot create a named employment relationship.

kind is role, correction or unknown. role_kind is executive, board_director, controlling_owner, employee, founder, owner or unknown for roles, and empty for non-roles. Do not collapse advisory councils, boards of visitors, civic membership or non-governing advisory bodies into corporate board_director: preserve their literal membership title and use unknown when the coarse taxonomy cannot express it. Reference only the organization candidate whose stated scope the text supports; a council is not silently the parent institute or corporation. Founding does not prove ownership, and ownership does not prove control. Omit degrees, awards, acquisition targets, visits and other non-role activity.

status is asserted, denied, hypothetical or unclear: the source's claim, not verified truth. A conditional future appointment is hypothetical. Preserve negated roles as denied, not affirmative membership. Corrections are separate interpretations: retracts links a correction to only the role it withdraws. contradicts links genuinely incompatible claims about the same entities/role/time, not successive jobs. No automatic or transitive correction chains. Empty context_links is valid.
