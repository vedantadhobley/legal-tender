# Receiver-flow committee identity coverage v1

This calculation publishes one exact-ID evidence decision for every committee
referenced by the receiver-reported flow graph but absent from its selected
cycle committee master.

`historical_registration` preserves official historical assertions without
promoting a historical name or attribute to the selected cycle.
`alternate_release_registration` preserves different-release same-cycle
evidence. `unresolved_reported_id` states that the reported ID was absent from
all explicitly audited masters. All three states are terminal-identity-
ineligible.

The calculation identity binds the shipped v1 flow bundle, its calculation and
selected master, every normalized comparison fact set, and every raw official
archive digest and storage key. Candidate linkage and summary facts are audit
diagnostics and do not affect this calculation's identity.
